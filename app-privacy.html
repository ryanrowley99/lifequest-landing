Privacy Policy

LifeQuest — mobile app
Last updated: September 2026

This covers the LifeQuest mobile app. The website privacy policy is here and covers this site only.

This policy explains what LifeQuest collects when you use the app, how, why, who else sees it, and what you can do about it. We've written it in plain English on purpose — and there's one section, "Your conversations with the coach", that we'd genuinely like you to read rather than scroll past.

Who we are

LifeQuest is run by Ryan Rowley, a sole trader based in the United Kingdom. For the purposes of UK GDPR, we are the "data controller" — meaning we decide what happens to your data and we're accountable for it.

Questions, requests, or complaints: ryanlrowley99@gmail.com

What we collect

Your account details. Your first name, your email address, and a securely hashed password. We never see or store your password in a readable form.

Your onboarding answers. During setup you tell the coach what you're working towards, where you're starting from, what you won't sacrifice, and — if you choose to — where your current path leads if nothing changes. These are stored and used to shape how the coach talks to you.

Your conversations with the coach. Everything you type to your coach, and everything the coach replies. This is stored so your conversations continue across sessions rather than starting fresh each time, and so the coach can refer back to what you've told it.

Your goals and progress. The life areas (pillars) you choose to work on, the skills and quests you set, what you complete, what you skip, and any reflections, mood or effort notes you add when you log something.

Your device timezone. Used to work out which calendar day a log belongs to, so "today" means your today.

Basic usage information. Message counts, timestamps, and whether requests to the coach succeeded or failed. Used to enforce daily limits and to spot faults.

We do not collect your location, your contacts, your photos, or any advertising identifiers. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

How we collect it

  • Directly from you, as you use the app — when you create your account, answer the onboarding questions, message the coach, set or complete a quest, or log how something went.
  • Automatically, from the app itself — timestamps, message counts, success/failure of coach requests, and your device's timezone. This happens in the background as you use the app; there is no separate tracking technology and no third-party analytics SDK.

We do not buy data about you from anyone, and we do not combine your data with data from other sources.

How we use your data

  • To run your account — sign you in, keep you signed in, and remember that you have completed onboarding.
  • To generate the coach's replies — your messages, your onboarding answers, your active skills and quests, and your recent activity are sent to Anthropic (see the next section) so the coach can respond in context.
  • To keep your conversations continuous — stored messages and a short running summary let the coach pick up where you left off instead of restarting each session.
  • To show you your progress — your logs and completions drive the numbers on the You screen and the state of your skill tree.
  • To keep the app usable and fair — timestamps and message counts enforce the daily coach-message limit.
  • To keep the app working — success/failure records and redacted monitoring copies (see below) help us find and fix faults and understand what the app costs to run.
  • To meet our legal obligations — responding to data requests, and keeping the minimum records needed to show we handled your consent properly.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell or rent it, and we do not use your conversations to train any AI model — and neither do our providers.

Your conversations with the coach — please read this bit

LifeQuest is a coaching app, so you may end up writing things about your health, your mental health, your relationships, your finances, or your beliefs. Under UK GDPR some of that counts as "special category" data, which gets extra protection.

Three things you should know:

  1. Your messages are sent to Anthropic to generate the coach's replies. They are processed to produce a response and are not used to train Anthropic's models.
  2. A redacted copy is sent to Langfuse, a service we use to monitor whether the app is working properly and what it costs to run. Before anything is sent, we automatically remove email addresses, phone numbers, and long numeric sequences such as card or account numbers. This redaction is automatic and imperfect — it removes patterns, not meaning. The substance of what you write is visible to us in those records.
  3. LifeQuest is not a medical service. Your coach is an AI, not a therapist, doctor, or crisis service. If you are struggling with your mental health, please speak to a qualified professional. If you are in crisis in the UK, Samaritans are available free on 116 123, any time.

Please don't share anything with your coach you would be uncomfortable being stored — including other people's personal details.

You can turn AI data-sharing off at any time in the app (You → Coach data sharing). While it is off, the coach cannot reply — it has nothing to send — but your quests, skills and history stay exactly as they are, and you can turn it back on whenever you want. You do not have to delete your account to withdraw this consent.

Our legal basis for using your data

  • To provide the app (your account, your conversations, your progress) — performance of our contract with you.
  • To keep the app working and secure (error logs, rate limits, monitoring) — our legitimate interests in running a reliable service.
  • To process special category information you choose to share — your explicit consent, which you give before your first coach message and which you can withdraw at any time from the You screen, or by deleting your account.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and everything in it — for as long as your account exists.
  • After you delete your account — removed from our live systems immediately, and from encrypted backups within 90 days, after which it is gone.
  • Monitoring records (redacted) — we do not keep these longer than we need them for reliability and cost analysis; they are pruned regularly and never retained beyond the life of your account.
  • Consent records — the dates you gave or withdrew AI data-sharing consent, and which version of this disclosure you agreed to, kept for as long as your account exists so we can show the consent was handled properly. These contain no message content.

Getting a copy of your data

You can export your data yourself, at any time, from inside the app: You → Export my data. This produces a single file containing your profile, your onboarding answers, your skills and progress, your quests, your logs, and your full history with the coach, which you can save or send wherever you like.

If you'd rather we sent it to you, email ryanlrowley99@gmail.com and we'll respond within one month, at no charge.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app at any time: You → Delete account. You'll be asked to confirm twice, because it cannot be undone.

When you confirm, we immediately and permanently delete:

  • your profile and onboarding answers;
  • every skill, quest, and log;
  • your entire conversation history with the coach, and the running summary built from it;
  • your account itself and your ability to sign in.

This is a genuine deletion, not a hidden or deactivated account. It is removed from our live systems at once and from encrypted backups within 90 days. We keep no copy afterwards, and there is nothing for us to restore if you change your mind.

If you'd rather we did it for you, email ryanlrowley99@gmail.com.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us to: give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or send it to another provider. You can do the first and third of those yourself in the app (above). You can also withdraw consent at any time, and complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Email ryanlrowley99@gmail.com and we'll respond within one month. There's no charge.

Children

LifeQuest is not intended for anyone under 18 and we don't knowingly collect data from under-18s. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by our providers. Your login session is stored in your device's secure keychain. Access to the production database is restricted to the app's own backend — the app on your phone never holds a key that could read anyone else's data — and users cannot read one another's conversations, something we test rather than assume.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above and, for anything significant, tell you in the app before it takes effect. If we ever add a new provider that receives your data, or start using your data for a new purpose, we'll ask for your consent again rather than carry the old one forward.
