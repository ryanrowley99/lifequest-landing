Privacy Policy

Questree — mobile app
Last updated: October 2026

This covers the Questree mobile app. The website privacy policy is here and covers this site only.

This policy explains what Questree collects when you use the app, how, why, who else sees it, and what you can do about it. We've written it in plain English on purpose — and there's one section, "What the AI sees", that we'd genuinely like you to read rather than scroll past.

Who we are

Questree is run by Rowley Labs Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company no. 17527212), registered office 42 Beaumaris Road, Bournemouth, BH11 9FX. For the purposes of UK GDPR, we are the "data controller" — meaning we decide what happens to your data and we're accountable for it.

Questions, requests, or complaints: support@getquestree.com

What we collect

Your account details. Your first name, your email address, and a securely hashed password. We never see or store your password in a readable form.

Your setup answers. During setup you tell Questree what you're working towards, where you're starting from and which areas of life you want to focus on. These are stored and used to suggest quests and skills that fit you.

What you type when creating quests. If you describe a quest in your own words, that text is stored with the quest and used to suggest a version that fits you.

Your goals and progress. The life areas (pillars) you choose to work on, the skills and quests you set, what you complete, what you skip, and your daily check-in ratings and any optional notes you add to them, and the XP and skill points you earn.

Your device timezone. Used to work out which calendar day a log belongs to, so "today" means your today.

Basic usage information. Timestamps, request counts, and whether requests to the AI succeeded or failed. Used to enforce fair-use limits and to spot faults.

Your reminder time. If you turn on the evening reminder, the time you choose is stored on your phone only. Reminders are scheduled on your device; we do not send push notifications from a server.

We do not collect your location, your contacts, your photos, or any advertising identifiers. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

How we collect it

  • Directly from you, as you use the app — when you create your account, answer the setup questions, create, set or complete a quest, or log how something went.
  • Automatically, from the app itself — timestamps, request counts, success/failure of AI requests, and your device's timezone. This happens in the background as you use the app; there is no separate tracking technology and no third-party analytics SDK.

We do not buy data about you from anyone, and we do not combine your data with data from other sources.

How we use your data

  • To run your account — sign you in, keep you signed in, and remember that you have completed setup.
  • To personalise your quests and skills — what you type during setup and when you create a quest is sent to Anthropic (see the next section) so it can suggest quests and skills that fit you. Your name and email are never sent.
  • To show you your progress — your logs and completions drive your XP, levels, the You screen and your skill tree.
  • To keep the app usable and fair — timestamps and request counts enforce fair-use limits on AI requests.
  • To keep the app working — success/failure records and redacted monitoring copies (see below) help us find and fix faults and understand what the app costs to run.
  • To meet our legal obligations — responding to data requests, and keeping the minimum records needed to show we handled your consent properly.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell or rent it, and we do not use what you write to train any AI model — and neither do our providers.

What the AI sees — please read this bit

Questree helps you work on your life, so you may end up writing things about your health, your mental health, your relationships, your finances, or your beliefs. Under UK GDPR some of that counts as "special category" data, which gets extra protection.

Three things you should know:

  1. What you type during setup and when creating quests is sent to Anthropic (the company behind Claude) to suggest quests and skills that fit you. Your name and email are never included. It is processed to produce a suggestion and are not used to train Anthropic's models.
  2. A redacted copy is sent to Langfuse, a service we use to monitor whether the app is working properly and what it costs to run. Before anything is sent, we automatically remove email addresses, phone numbers, and long numeric sequences such as card or account numbers. This redaction is automatic and imperfect — it removes patterns, not meaning. The substance of what you write is visible to us in those records.
  3. Questree is not a medical service. Questree's suggestions come from an AI, not a therapist, doctor, or crisis service. If you are struggling with your mental health, please speak to a qualified professional. If you are in crisis in the UK, Samaritans are available free on 116 123, any time.

Please don't type anything you would be uncomfortable being stored — including other people's personal details.

You can turn AI data-sharing off at any time in the app (Menu → Your data → AI data sharing). While it is off, nothing is sent to Anthropic and you won't get personalised suggestions, but you can still pick quests from the library, and your quests, skills and history stay exactly as they are, and you can turn it back on whenever you want. You do not have to delete your account to withdraw this consent.

Our legal basis for using your data

  • To provide the app (your account, your quests, your progress) — performance of our contract with you.
  • To keep the app working and secure (error logs, rate limits, monitoring) — our legitimate interests in running a reliable service.
  • To process special category information you choose to share — your explicit consent, which you give during setup before anything is sent to the AI, and which you can withdraw at any time from Menu → Your data, or by deleting your account.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and everything in it — for as long as your account exists.
  • After you delete your account — removed from our live systems immediately, and from encrypted backups within 90 days, after which it is gone.
  • Monitoring records (redacted) — we do not keep these longer than we need them for reliability and cost analysis; they are pruned regularly and never retained beyond the life of your account.
  • Consent records — the dates you gave or withdrew AI data-sharing consent, and which version of this disclosure you agreed to, kept for as long as your account exists so we can show the consent was handled properly. These contain no message content.

Getting a copy of your data

You can export your data yourself, at any time, from inside the app: Menu → Your data → Export my data. This produces a single file containing your profile, your setup answers, your skills and progress, your quests, your logs and check-ins, which you can save or send wherever you like.

If you'd rather we sent it to you, email support@getquestree.com and we'll respond within one month, at no charge.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app at any time: Menu → Account → Delete account. You'll be asked to confirm twice, because it cannot be undone.

When you confirm, we immediately and permanently delete:

  • your profile and setup answers;
  • every skill, quest, and log;
  • every check-in and note, and everything you typed during setup;
  • your account itself and your ability to sign in.

This is a genuine deletion, not a hidden or deactivated account. It is removed from our live systems at once and from encrypted backups within 90 days. We keep no copy afterwards, and there is nothing for us to restore if you change your mind.

If you'd rather we did it for you, email support@getquestree.com.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us to: give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or send it to another provider. You can do the first and third of those yourself in the app (above). You can also withdraw consent at any time, and complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Email support@getquestree.com and we'll respond within one month. There's no charge.

Children

Questree is not intended for anyone under 18 and we don't knowingly collect data from under-18s. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by our providers. Your login session is stored in your device's secure keychain. Access to the production database is restricted to the app's own backend — the app on your phone never holds a key that could read anyone else's data — and users cannot read one another's data, something we test rather than assume.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above and, for anything significant, tell you in the app before it takes effect. If we ever add a new provider that receives your data, or start using your data for a new purpose, we'll ask for your consent again rather than carry the old one forward.
