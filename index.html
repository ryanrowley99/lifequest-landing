Questions? Answers.

When can I get it?

Early access opens in December on iPhone. Join the waitlist and we'll email you the day it's live.

Is it on Android?

Not yet. Questree is iPhone only for now.

How is it different from a habit tracker or Habitica?

It starts with one goal, not a list of habits. Your daily quests are built around it, and a timeline shows how far you are from your target date. Miss a week and nothing resets.

What happens if I miss a few days?

Nothing bad. There is no streak to break. Questree counts the days you showed up, not the days you missed. When you come back, a card shows the progress you have made so far, and you carry on.

Do I have to set everything up myself?

No. Pick your main objective and Questree suggests quests that fit. Change them or pick your own from the library.

What do you do with my data?

Your quests and check-ins are stored securely in the EU. What you type when setting up or creating a quest is sent to Anthropic to suggest quests that fit you, never with your name or email, and never used to train AI. You can switch that off, and delete your account and everything in it, from inside the app. We never sell your data. Full details on our App privacy page.
